# Bug tracker

Known defects of MapleStory 2 Offline: severity, how to reproduce, state. Living document;
fixed defects stay listed with their fix. The full fix history per release is in [../ADAPTATIONS.md](../ADAPTATIONS.md);
data findings come from the read-only sweep (`Maple2.Server.Tests/Data/DataSweepTest.cs`, R12-04).

Severity: **critical** (data loss, crash, progression blocked for everyone), **high** (a feature or content piece does
not work), **medium** (works with a visible flaw or a workaround), **low** (cosmetic, rare, or log noise).
