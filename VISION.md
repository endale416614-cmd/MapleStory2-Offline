# Project Vision

My goal is to make the game 100% playable and give players full control over their game, characters and progress.

## 1. 100% Playable

- All Epic Quests, dungeons and bosses fully playable.
- Top priority before anything else.

## 2. Korean Edition

- Full translation and conversion of Korean content.
- Merge the Korean and Global editions into one complete game.

## 3. Quality of Life (QoL)

- Refine existing systems for a smoother experience.
- Community feedback and suggestions are welcome.

## 4. Co-op wLAN RPG (not an MMORPG)

This is not about turning the game back into an MMORPG. The goal is a co-op RPG you play with friends over wLAN (your
home network) or a server you choose, the way Stardew Valley's co-op works.

In an MMORPG your character lives on someone else's server and disappears when that server shuts down. Here your
character is a save file on your own PC, and other people's games are just places you visit.

The game already supports multiplayer through the existing emulator; the work is to rebuild it around these rules:

- Local saves: your character and progress stay on your PC.
- Bring your character: join a server with your offline character and return home with everything you've earned.
- Offline-first: solo play always works without a server.
- Flexible hosting: play on a friend's PC or an always-on community server.
- Compatibility checks: servers verify game versions, data hashes and mod lists when joining.

I already have the multiplayer foundation to build on, but 100% playability comes first.

## 5. Modding Tools

- Provide tools for players to customize, modify and expand their own game.

## The Bigger Picture

I want you to own your game and your own OC. No more losing years of progress because someone else's server shuts down
or deletes your character.

Your character stays on your PC. Servers are just places to play together, not where your character's existence
depends on someone else.

**Own your game. Own your character. Keep your progress.**
